### Featured

**[RAG Hyperparameter Lab](https://github.com/saisrinivas7/rag-hyperparameter-lab)**
Finds the RAG settings that actually work on your documents. Sweeps embedding models,
chunking strategies, overlap and retrieval modes against your own corpus and ranks them
against labelled questions. Gold answers are stored as character spans rather than chunk
IDs, so no chunking configuration gets a free win from having produced the labels.
Visual pipeline canvas, and it runs entirely offline on local models.

**[Chicago Accident Analysis](https://github.com/saisrinivas7/Chicago_Accident_Analysis)**
Severity modelling on Chicago traffic collision data — classification with and without
resampling, red-light camera impact, and inferential statistics against neighbourhood
poverty rates.

**[Stock Prices with LSTMs](https://github.com/saisrinivas7/Stock-Prices-using-LSTMs)**
Forecasting out-of-sample directional movements of S&P 500 constituents from 1993 to 2018
for intraday trading.
